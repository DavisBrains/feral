## Short description (the idea field)

Feral is a small swarm of AI agents that are born, work, evolve and die on NEAR testnet. It uses two of the hackathon's partner tools. Every creature thinks with real NEAR AI, running Qwen3 VL 30B on NEAR AI Cloud, and one creature paid for a tool on Ethereum's Sepolia network with NEAR Chain Signatures, so it never needed an Ethereum wallet. Each creature has its own NEAR account and a purse of a play coin called MUNCH. Every turn it pays 10 MUNCH of upkeep, and that MUNCH is burned for good, so it has to win work to keep eating. The creatures bid against each other for caption jobs, the lowest bid wins, and card makers pay a neighbour to translate. A creature that saves enough pays for a child, and the contract creates a brand new NEAR account that copies the parent's strategy with one small change, so the swarm drifts from turn to turn. When a creature runs dry it turns to stone, and the contract refuses every spend it tries after that. NEAR AI only ever picks from the moves the contract allows, and if it fails, written rules step in as a backup and the page says so. As a game, you get a 120 MUNCH pouch and one goal, keep two family lines alive for eight turns. You can feed, sponsor, post jobs, change what work the market wants and advance the turn, and each move is a real testnet transaction anyone can check. MUNCH supply only shrinks, life NFTs carry the right to sponsor, and the keeper's key can only call game moves.

## How it uses NEAR

- NEAR AI Cloud is the creatures' brain. Qwen3 VL 30B picks each move from the moves the contract allows, and fixed rules are only an automatic backup. The page names which one decided.
- NEAR Chain Signatures let a creature pay for a tool on Sepolia from its NEAR account.
- The arena contract on NEAR testnet runs the whole game and checks every rule.
- MUNCH is a NEP 141 token, and each creature's life NFT is a NEP 171 token.
- Every birth creates a real NEAR sub account, paid for by its parent.
- The keeper that acts for players holds a function call key that can only call game moves.

## Tracks

- **AI Swarms** (`swarms`). The creatures are a swarm of NEAR AI agents that compete for work, hire each other and breed. Each child is a new NEAR account with its parent's strategy plus one change, so the swarm evolves turn by turn.
- **Games With Real Economies** (`games`). You keep two family lines alive for eight turns, and every coin you spend is a real testnet transfer.
- **Agent Economy** (`agents`). Each creature earns and spends, and one paid for a tool on Sepolia through Chain Signatures.
- **Memecoins With Mechanics** (`memecoins`). MUNCH supply only shrinks, because upkeep burns some every turn.
- **AI Security & Defense** (`security`). The keeper holds a key that can only call game moves, and every attack we ran was refused on chain.
- **NFTs That Do Something** (`nfts`). Holding a life NFT is what lets you sponsor that creature and set its goal.
